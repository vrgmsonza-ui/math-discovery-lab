# Competency coverage review

Reviewed 254 source weekly groups and 506 competency entries across Grades 1–10. All 300 grade–term–week slots remain represented. Original competency strings are unchanged.

## Findings and changes

Corrected companion-model assignments in 76 groups. Added 11 models for previously weak or missing representations: tens/ones regrouping, composite figures, pyramids with different bases, absolute-value equations, functions/domain/range, sampling, frequency/position/variability, scientific-notation operations, monomial operations, radical simplification/equations and triangle inequalities.

Expanded number-line precision to four decimal places, money denominations, rounding places and fixed time conversions. Removed lexical false positives such as an inequality number line for triangle inequalities and polygon geometry for frequency polygons.

## Limits that still need teacher-led completion

These are companion simulations, not evidence of full competency mastery. Every competency now has a classroom completion prompt, selectable inside the lab and presentation view.

- Geometry construction, instrument use, two-column proofs, cut-outs and physical models need learner-produced work.
- Word problems, multistep solutions, reading/writing number names, actual currency recognition and surveys need contextual tasks and assessment.
- Algebra tiles still illustrate a limited family of nonnegative binomial products; general polynomial factorization, arbitrary rational-expression operations and equations reducible to quadratics require worked examples beyond these controls.
- Radical equations are demonstrated by √(x+a)=b; this does not cover every radical equation or operation.
- Circle geometry includes sector/segment area and intersecting chords; external secant/tangent angle and length cases still require teacher diagrams and worked solutions.
- The statistics extension includes frequency tables, stem-and-leaf and cumulative frequency polygons; grouped cumulative histograms and all textbook quantile conventions are not implemented.
- Cube nets do not represent every solid net. Time-zone controls use fixed offsets rather than an interactive world map.
